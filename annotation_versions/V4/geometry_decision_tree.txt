GEOMETRY CLASSIFICATION ONLY

Before choosing a geometry label:
- Do not classify only from raw GeoJSON geometry type.
- First identify what spatial structure the dataset represents.
- Use geometry_type_counts, polygon_topology_hints, metadata, layer name, attributes, and MapDescription.
- The dataset summary may omit raw coordinates on purpose.
- Entity labels are not relevant in this step. Do not use ObjectDS/PatchDS reasoning for geometry.

Allowed Geometry labels:
- PlainVectorRegion
- PointDS
- VectorTessellation
- LineDS

==================================================
GEOMETRY CLASSIFICATION
==================================================

Q1. What kind of geometry does the dataset contain?

A. Points or icons
B. Lines that are not part of polygon boundaries
C. Regions / polygons / areas

If A -> GEOMETRY = PointDS
If B -> GEOMETRY = LineDS
If C -> continue to Region Geometry questions

--------------------------------------------------
REGION GEOMETRY QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Q2. Do the regions form, or are they intended to form, a complete or near-complete partition of the study area?

Typical examples:
- neighbourhoods
- districts
- boroughs
- postcode areas
- census/statistical units
- administrative areas
- grid cells
- land-use polygons
- zoning polygons
- area-wide thematic classifications

Yes -> GEOMETRY = VectorTessellation
No  -> continue

Q3. Are the polygons gridded, tiled, or pixelated units that cover the area?

Yes -> GEOMETRY = VectorTessellation
No  -> GEOMETRY = PlainVectorRegion

--------------------------------------------------
POLYGON TOPOLOGY HINTS
--------------------------------------------------

If dataset_summary.polygon_topology_hints exists, use it as supporting evidence only:
- High shared_boundary_ratio, many adjacent_polygon_pairs, low overlap_ratio, and high convex_hull_fill_ratio support VectorTessellation.
- Low shared_boundary_ratio, few adjacent_polygon_pairs, low convex_hull_fill_ratio, or isolated polygons support PlainVectorRegion.
- Treat suggested_region_geometry as a hint, not as a mandatory label. Override it when metadata, layer name, attributes, or dataset meaning clearly indicate another geometry label.
